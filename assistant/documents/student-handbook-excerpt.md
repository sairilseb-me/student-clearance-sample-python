# Student Handbook — Clearance Policy Excerpt

Clearance is required at the end of every semester for all students, and must be completed by routing your clearance form to each office in the assigned order: Adviser, Library, Treasurer, and Guidance Office.

Clearance requests are processed on a rolling basis throughout the last two weeks of the semester. Students are strongly encouraged not to wait until the final exam period, since office queues get significantly longer closer to the deadline.

Students graduating this semester follow a different, stricter timeline: graduating students must complete full clearance at least two weeks before the scheduled commencement ceremony, not just before the end of the semester. In addition to the standard four offices, graduating students must also route their clearance through the Registrar's Office for a diploma hold check — this confirms there are no outstanding academic or administrative holds that would prevent the release of your diploma.

If any office rejects your clearance request, the rejection reason will be shown on your dashboard along with remarks from the approver. You may resubmit to that specific office once the issue is resolved — you do not need to restart the entire clearance process from scratch.

Only after every required office (and, for graduating students, the Registrar's Office) has signed off will your clearance be marked fully approved and your final signed clearance document become available for download.
