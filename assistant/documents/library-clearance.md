# Library Clearance Requirements

To get library clearance, you must return every book, journal, or other material currently checked out under your student ID. The library will not sign your clearance form while any item remains unreturned, regardless of how minor the item is.

You must also settle any outstanding fines before clearance can be granted. Fines accrue at five pesos per day per overdue item. You can check your current fine balance at the library circulation desk or through the library's online portal.

Once your books are returned and fines are paid, bring your clearance form to the library circulation desk during office hours (Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM). The librarian on duty will inspect your account and sign off directly on the form if everything is in order.

If you believe you've returned a book that still shows as borrowed in the system, bring your official return receipt — the library keeps physical return slips for exactly this kind of dispute.
