# Clearance FAQ

**Q: How long does the whole clearance process take?**
A: If there are no rejections and you clear offices promptly, most students complete clearance within a few days. Waiting until the last week of the semester will take longer due to office queues.

**Q: Can I clear offices in any order?**
A: No. Clearance requests are routed in a fixed sequence — Adviser, Library, Treasurer, then Guidance Office (plus the Registrar's Office for graduating students). You can't skip ahead to an office before the ones before it in the sequence have signed.

**Q: What happens if one office rejects my request?**
A: The clearance request is marked rejected and paused at that office. You'll see the rejection remarks on your dashboard, and you can resubmit once you've resolved the issue — you don't need to restart from the beginning.

**Q: Who do I contact if I have unpaid fees?**
A: The Treasurer's Office handles all unpaid tuition, miscellaneous fees, and laboratory fees. Go there in person with your student ID to settle your balance or arrange a payment plan.

**Q: I'm graduating this semester — is there anything different for me?**
A: Yes. Graduating students must complete clearance at least two weeks before commencement (earlier than the standard end-of-semester deadline) and must also clear through the Registrar's Office for a diploma hold check, in addition to the usual four offices.

**Q: Where do I download my final signed clearance?**
A: Once every required office has signed, your dashboard will show your clearance as fully approved and give you a link to view/download the final signed document.
