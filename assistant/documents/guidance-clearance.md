# Guidance Office Clearance Requirements

The Guidance Office clears students on behavioral and counseling matters. For most students, this is a formality confirming there are no unresolved disciplinary cases on file.

If you were referred to guidance counseling at any point in the semester, you must complete any required follow-up sessions before the Guidance Office will sign your clearance. Incomplete counseling requirements are the most common reason guidance clearance gets delayed.

Bring your student ID when clearing with guidance. The counselor on duty will check your file and sign immediately if there are no open cases or pending requirements.

Transferees and returning students (those who took a leave of absence) should also bring their prior clearance record if available, since the Guidance Office keeps continuous behavioral records across semesters.
