# Treasurer's Office Clearance Requirements

The Treasurer's Office handles all financial matters related to clearance, including unpaid tuition, miscellaneous fees, laboratory fees, and any other outstanding balance on your student account.

If you have unpaid fees, you must go directly to the Treasurer's Office (Room 104, Admin Building) to settle your balance or arrange a payment plan before clearance will be signed. The Treasurer's Office does not accept balance disputes through email — you need to appear in person with your student ID.

Once your balance is fully settled, or an approved payment plan is on file, the treasurer will sign your clearance form on the spot. Keep your official receipt, since it may be requested again during enrollment for the following semester.

Scholars and grantees should bring their scholarship or grant certificate when clearing, since some fee categories may already be covered and shouldn't be charged twice.
