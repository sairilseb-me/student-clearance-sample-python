# Adviser Clearance Requirements

Your academic adviser signs off on clearance to confirm your enrollment and academic standing are in order for the semester. This is typically the first office in the clearance sequence, since other offices may rely on your adviser having already confirmed your official class list.

Before approaching your adviser, make sure your enrollment is finalized (no pending adjustments, added, or dropped subjects) and that you have no unresolved incomplete (INC) grades from prior semesters that require action this term.

Advisers hold consultation hours posted outside their faculty office door. If you can't find your adviser during those hours, the department secretary can help you schedule a short appointment — clearance sign-offs usually take less than five minutes once your records are confirmed.

If you're unsure who your assigned adviser is, check with the department office; adviser assignments are based on your program and year level and may change between semesters.
